Reading aloud turns idle moments into reading time. A short walk becomes a chapter; a commute becomes an essay. The overlay stays out of the page's way until you ask for it.

A content script extracts the article into speakable chunks, a hybrid text-to-speech engine speaks them, and an as-you-read highlighter tracks the active sentence or paragraph.

The Web Speech API is the primary engine; when it reports no voices, a bundled WASM Piper model takes over so the reader works even where system voices are absent.
